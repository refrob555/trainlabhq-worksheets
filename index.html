The Park · Age 7

Sam and his dog Max went to the park. The sun was bright and the grass was green. Sam threw a red ball. Max ran fast and brought the ball back. They played until it was time to go home. Sam gave Max a big hug. They were both happy.

Sound it out
  • bright → br · ight
  • grass → gr · ass
  • threw → thr · ew
  • brought → br · ought
  • until → un · til
  • happy → hap · py

Questions: Who is Max? What color was the ball? Where did they go? How did they feel?

Answers for grown-ups: Max is Sam’s dog. The ball was red. They went to the park. They felt happy.
